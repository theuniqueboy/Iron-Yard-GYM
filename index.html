FAQ

Common questions

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes. Fill in the free trial form below and WhatsApp will open with your message already written.

What are the gym timings?

Morning 5:00 AM to 10:00 AM and evening 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM. The ladies' batch runs from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. On Sundays, only the morning batch runs.

Do you have a ladies' batch?

Yes. The ladies' batch runs from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and Yoga & Zumba also has a separate ladies' batch.

Do I get a workout plan or diet chart?

Every plan includes a workout plan. The Quarterly plan adds a diet chart and a monthly body check, and Personal Training includes a one-on-one coach, a diet chart and a monthly progress check.

Can I pause my membership?

The Yearly plan includes a 15-day plan freeze.

How do I pay?

Tap Pay via UPI on any plan, or message us on WhatsApp if you have questions.
